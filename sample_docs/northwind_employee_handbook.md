# Northwind Analytics — Employee Handbook (Fictional sample document)

*This handbook belongs to a fictional company and exists only to demo DocuMind.*

## 1. Working hours

Core working hours at Northwind Analytics are 10:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may plan their own schedule as long as they work 40 hours per week. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without the attendee's agreement.

## 2. Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried over to the next year; any days above 10 lapse on 31 March.

Interns receive 1 day of paid leave per month of internship. Interns cannot carry leave over.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave: every employee gets 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave: primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid leave; secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid leave. Parental leave must be requested at least 30 days in advance where possible.

Leave requests are submitted in the PeopleHub portal and approved by the reporting manager. Requests for 5 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 3. Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing their 90-day probation period. Fully remote arrangements need approval from both the reporting manager and the HR business partner.

The company provides a one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, claimable within 6 months of joining. Internet costs are reimbursed up to INR 1,000 per month for employees who work remotely at least 2 days per week.

## 4. Expenses and travel

All business expenses must be submitted in the Expensely tool within 30 days of being incurred, with a receipt for any item above INR 500.

Travel rules:
- Domestic flights are booked in economy class through the travel desk.
- Hotel stays are capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night elsewhere.
- The daily meal allowance during travel is INR 1,200.
- Cabs to and from the airport are reimbursed at actual cost; app-based cabs are preferred.

Expenses above INR 25,000 need written pre-approval from a department head.

## 5. Equipment and IT

Every employee receives a laptop on their first day. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours by emailing it-help@northwind.example.

Personal software may not be installed on company laptops without IT approval. Company data must never be stored on personal USB drives.

## 6. Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, a manager assessment and goals for the next six months. Salary revisions are decided once a year, after the April review, and take effect from 1 July.

## 7. Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 30,000 for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over. Certification exam fees are reimbursed only if the exam is passed.

## 8. Code of conduct

Northwind expects respectful behaviour from everyone. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated and can be reported confidentially to ethics@northwind.example. Reports are acknowledged within 2 working days.
